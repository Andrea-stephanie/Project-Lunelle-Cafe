🌸 Brand Story & History

How LUNELLE Café Began

“Somewhere between the pages of a favourite novel and a glass of strawberry matcha, there is a little place called LUNELLE Café. Created by a girl who finds happiness in quiet mornings, fictional stories, pretty little things, and meaningful conversations. LUNELLE is a cozy little escape for everyone. Whether you come here to read your favourite book, enjoy a cup of matcha, spend time with someone special, or simply have a moment to yourself, there's always a little space for you.”

Why It Was Established: Started as a passionate local UMKM concept in 2026, LUNELLE Café was created to fill a gentle need—a café that isn't loud or rushed, where introverts feel completely at ease sitting alone with a novel, while close friends can still share laughter over sweet desserts.

✨ Our Vision

A Sanctuary for Quiet Joy & Creativity

To become the most beloved pastel book café and local creative sanctuary where every cup, pastry, and book shelf inspires calm mornings, meaningful friendships, and everyday storytelling.

🎀 Our Mission

Crafted with Care & Soft Details

  • Serve homemade strawberry matcha, specialty coffee, and freshly baked pastries using quality local ingredients.
  • Provide a peaceful, aesthetic reading and study space through The Lunelle Reading Corner.
  • Support student creativity, book clubs, and local community gatherings.
