📚 Who We Are

A Warm Circle for Students & Creatives

LUNELLE Creative Community is a student-centered creative and literacy community supported by LUNELLE Café. We bring together university students and young creatives who love reading fiction, journaling, illustration, and meaningful peer discussions.

Background & History: Born in early 2026 from informal weekend reading sessions at The Lunelle Reading Corner, a small group of students realized how comforting it was to share books and creative hobbies in a welcoming, non-judgmental space. Today, the community hosts monthly literacy circles, study-together sessions, and collaborative campus workshops.

✨ Community Vision

Inspiring Expressive & Empathetic Youth

To cultivate a supportive, inclusive student creative community where reading habits, artistic expression, and genuine friendship flourish side by side.

🎀 Community Mission

Learn, Share & Grow Together

  • Encourage recreational reading and thoughtful book discussions among students.
  • Provide accessible workshops in journaling, visual design, and personal development.
  • Build positive collaborations between student organizations and local creative UMKM.

🗓️ What We Do

Programs & Regular Activities

1. Monthly Book Club

Themed fiction reading circles every last Friday of the month with custom ribbon bookmarks and discussion prompts.

2. Creative Journaling

Scrapbooking, reading log decoration, and mindful reflection sessions using pastel stationery and washi tapes.

3. Student Gathering

Cross-faculty networking and co-studying afternoons designed to help students make new friends comfortably.

4. Coffee & Conversation

Small-group sharing circles on personal growth, university life, career dreams, and mental wellness.

5. Community Workshop

Skill-sharing classes ranging from watercolor illustration and digital design to beginner beverage crafting.
